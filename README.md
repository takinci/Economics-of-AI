# Who Pays for Radiology AI?

**Live site: https://takinci.github.io/Economics-of-AI/**

A world tour of 18 stops on how radiology AI is paid for, who pays, and who keeps the value it creates.

## What is on the site

A long-form, interactive briefing, in the order of the talk:

- **The bet:** why the economic value of radiology AI is the open question.
- **The evidence:** what the economic evaluations show so far.
- **The world tour:** a filterable map of 18 stops across Oceania, Asia, Europe, Africa, South America and the United States, filtered by payment archetype and by whether a dedicated fee exists.
- **What to do next:** a framework, precedents, recommendations and takeaways.
- **Sources:** the full reference list, grouped by region.

## Background

The page is based on:

- the talk "Economic Value of Radiology AI: A World Tour" (EuSoMII Annual Meeting 2026);
- the talk "The Global View: How AI Pricing and Reimbursement Differ Outside the US" (RSNA 2026);
- the paper "Who Pays for Radiology AI? A Comparative Review of Reimbursement Models Across 18 Health Systems" (in preparation).

Payment policy changes often. Status is as of October 2026; check the cited sources before acting on any figure.

## Running locally

The site is a single self-contained page ([index.html](index.html)) with no build step. Open it in a browser. Web fonts load from Google Fonts, so an internet connection is needed for the intended typography.

## Deployment

Published with GitHub Pages through the workflow in [.github/workflows/jekyll-gh-pages.yml](.github/workflows/jekyll-gh-pages.yml). Every push to `main` redeploys the site.
